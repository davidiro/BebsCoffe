OUR MISSION

At Bebs Coffee, our mission is to create a space where community, comfort, and quality come together in every cup. We believe a coffee shop should be more than a place to grab a drink it should be a welcoming environment where people slow down, connect, and feel at home. Every detail in our café, from the beans we source to the way we greet our guests, reflects our commitment to hospitality and craftsmanship. We partner with ethical growers who share our passion for sustainability, ensuring that every bean is cultivated with care and respect for the environment. Our drinks are handcrafted with intention, blending tradition with creativity to deliver flavors that feel familiar yet inspiring. Whether you're here for your morning ritual, a quiet moment of reflection, or a conversation with friends, we strive to make each visit memorable. Bebs Coffee is built on the belief that small moments matter — the warmth of a fresh brew, the comfort of a friendly smile, and the joy of a space designed for everyone. Our mission is simple: to serve exceptional coffee and create a community where people feel seen, valued, and welcomed every single day.

OUR FOUNDER

Alex Rivera, founder of Bebs Coffee, built the shop around a love for handcrafted drinks and genuine community.
With a focus on ethically sourced beans and warm hospitality,
Alex's mission is simple: serve great coffee and create a space where everyone feels welcome.
